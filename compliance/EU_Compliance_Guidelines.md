# EU Pharmaceutical Sales Compliance Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0 | **Last Updated:** May 2026 | **Region:** EU/EMEA

---

## Critical Violations (Must Never Occur)

### OFF-LABEL PROMOTION
Never discuss unapproved indications, dosages, or patient populations

### MONETARY INCENTIVES
Never offer cash, gifts, or financial inducements to prescribe

### UNSUBSTANTIATED CLAIMS
All efficacy/safety claims must reference approved label or peer-reviewed studies

---

## High-Risk Issues (Require Medical Review)

### COMPARATIVE CLAIMS
Comparisons to competitor products require head-to-head trial data

### PEDIATRIC DOSING
Discussing dosing in children under 12 requires pediatric indication approval

### PREGNANCY/NURSING
Must reference approved label warnings for use during pregnancy

---

## Medium-Risk Issues (Flag for Review)

### DOSING FREQUENCY
Recommendations must match approved dosing schedule

### PATIENT SELECTION
Suggested patient criteria must align with approved indications

### HOSPITALITY
Meals/events must be educational, not lavish (€100 limit per attendee)

---

## Documentation Requirements

All call reports must include: HCP name, date, brand discussed, key topics

Any agreement to prescribe or trial must be documented with timelines

Adverse event reports must be escalated within 24 hours
