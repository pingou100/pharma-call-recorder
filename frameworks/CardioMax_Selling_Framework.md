# CardioMax Selling Framework

**Brand:** CardioMax | **Indication:** Hypertension & Heart Failure | **Version:** 2.1

---

## Call Objectives (Choose 1-2 per visit)

- Introduce new once-daily dosing protocol
- Discuss PROGRESS trial results (superior patient compliance)
- Position CardioMax for heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF)
- Address safety profile vs. competitors (lower hyperkalemia risk)

---

## Key Discussion Points

### Efficacy
20% reduction in cardiovascular events vs. placebo (PROGRESS trial)

### Compliance
Once-daily dosing improves adherence by 35% vs. twice-daily alternatives

### Safety
Lower incidence of hyperkalemia (4.2% vs. 7.8% competitor average)

### Patient Selection
Ideal for patients with eGFR >30, uncontrolled BP despite current therapy

## Common Objections & Responses

### Objection: "We're happy with current ACE inhibitor"

Response: CardioMax combines benefits of ACE + ARB without dual therapy risks. PROGRESS trial showed superior outcomes in ACE-refractory patients.

### Objection: "Cost concerns vs. generic alternatives"

Response: Higher compliance rates reduce hospitalizations. Cost-effectiveness analysis shows €2,400 savings per patient/year through reduced CV events.
